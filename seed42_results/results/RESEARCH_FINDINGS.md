## 7. Limitations

- **Single seed (42), single fold (0).** The test-Dice gaps between models (~0.01) are of the same order as typical seed-to-seed variance; multi-seed runs are needed for firm conclusions.
- λ values are the config defaults (boundary 0.3, consistency 1.0); Stage B tuning results were not used to select them.
- Model selection uses validation Dice only, which may favour region accuracy over boundary metrics.

See `failure_analysis.md` for failure modes and per-case changes.
